Our Story

Made for Moments
Worth Celebrating.

🌿 At Butter & Bliss Bakery, every cake begins with a simple belief: authentic ingredients, pure vegetarian goodness, and patient hands create the most memorable bakes. 🎂✨ 🏡 We’re a home bakery rooted in Cuttack, where every cake and sweet treat is homemade, handmade, freshly prepared, and crafted with genuine care — bringing warmth and comfort to every slice. 🧁💛

🎉 Whether it’s a layered birthday celebration, 💍 an anniversary surprise, 🎁 a thoughtful gift, or simply something to make an ordinary day feel special — we bake every order with the same love and attention we’d give to something made for our own family. 🌸🍰

  • Anniversaries & milestones
  • Gifting & special occasions
  • Custom orders, any design
